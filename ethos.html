01 / GRACE

We don't pretend the walk is easy. We stumble daily, sometimes hourly. But we were never made righteous by our own effort. We're made righteous through Christ. That's the whole point of grace; it was never something to earn.

Grace before performance.
02 / THE MAP

Scripture is clear on the what, for example, abstain from sexual immorality, forgive one another, repent, put off the old self. But the how rarely gets taught. Sermons preach the destination and at times skip the map. So people carry the weight of what they're supposed to do with no practical way to actually do it, and end up either performing faith they don't feel or quietly giving up.

03 / PRACTICE

That's what House of Yeshua is here for. Not another brand telling you to try harder, but practical tools in the form of guides, prompts, accountability, and products for the parts of faith that actually take repetition and work, not just conviction.
