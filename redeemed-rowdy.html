01 / RIGHTEOUS ≠ TIMID

You can be angry.
You just can't move mad.

Redemption does not erase personality, emotion, courage or backbone. It puts them under the authority of Christ. Anger is an emotion. Sin is a response.

Speak up.
Set the boundary.
Keep your character.

Call something what it is. Walk away if you need to. Just don't let somebody else's behaviour recruit you into behaving unlike Christ.

02 / WWJD? WELL...

He didn't break God's standard.
He broke theirs.

Jesus repeatedly crossed lines that prejudice, legalism and social convention had drawn around people. Tap a card for the biblical receipts.

Jesus flipped tables.

Righteous zeal was not passive. It was also not petty revenge.

He healed on the Sabbath.

Compassion over performative rule-keeping.

He spoke with the woman everyone avoided.

Jesus crossed a social and ethnic boundary others treated as fixed.

He ate with the people respectable crowds side-eyed.

Proximity to sinners did not compromise His holiness.

He confronted hypocrisy.

Gentleness and truth were never enemies.
